How We Extract Our Coffees

The same coffee bean tastes radically different depending on contact time, agitation, grind size, and water temperature. Here is how we calibrate our manual bar.

Hario V60 Pour-Over

Clean & Floral

The Physics: A conical dripper with 60-degree spiral ribs and thin paper filters allows rapid flow rate and pure flavor separation.

  • Brew Ratio: 1:16 (15g coffee to 240g water)
  • Water Temperature: 92°C
  • Grind Size: Medium-Fine (sea salt texture)
  • Cup Profile: Tea-like body, sparkling acidity, pristine origin clarity.

Inverted Aeropress

Sweet & Round

The Physics: Total immersion brewing followed by gentle pneumatic pressure pushes sweetness forward while keeping bitterness low.

  • Brew Ratio: 1:14 (17g coffee to 238g water)
  • Water Temperature: 88°C
  • Grind Size: Medium (table salt texture)
  • Cup Profile: Velvety body, concentrated fruit sweetness, balanced finish.

Kyoto 18-Hour Cold Drip

Rich & Low-Acid

The Physics: Chilled ice water drips through a compacted bed of ground coffee at a pace of 1 drop per 1.5 seconds over 18 hours.

  • Brew Ratio: 1:11 (80g coffee to 880ml iced water)
  • Temperature: 4°C – 6°C
  • Grind Size: Coarse
  • Cup Profile: Zero bitter tannins, pronounced molasses and berry notes.

Read more about the scientific differences in our guide: Coffee vs Cold Brew: Extraction & Chemistry.

La Marzocco Espresso

Dense & Textured

The Physics: 9 bars of hydraulic pressure forced through an 18-gram compressed puck of finely ground arabica in 28 to 32 seconds.

  • Brew Ratio: 1:2 (18g dry dose in, 36g liquid espresso out)
  • Temperature: 93.5°C
  • Grind Size: Very Fine
  • Cup Profile: Thick crema, intense aromatic oils, ideal standalone or with milk.
