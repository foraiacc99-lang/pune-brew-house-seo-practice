Daily Rituals

From Morning Sourdough to Afternoon Cold Drip

Good coffee deserves wholesome food. Our kitchen prepares breakfast all day, pairing fermented sourdough bread from local artisanal bakeries with fresh, vibrant toppings.

  • Signature Hass Avocado Toast with citrus zest and chili flakes
  • Classic Fluffy Scrambled Eggs on toasted country sourdough
  • Slow Drip Cold Brew steeped over 18 hours for natural chocolate sweetness
