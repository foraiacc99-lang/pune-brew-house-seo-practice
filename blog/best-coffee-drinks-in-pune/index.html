Best Coffee Drinks to Try in Pune: A Local Specialty Guide

By Arjun Kulkarni • Published: September 10, 2026 • 6 min read

Pune's coffee culture has experienced a profound renaissance over the past decade. While the city's beloved heritage Irani cafes and traditional South Indian filter coffee joints will forever remain dear to our hearts, a new generation of coffee enthusiasts across neighborhoods like Koregaon Park, FC Road, and Viman Nagar are seeking out drinks that highlight the botanical nuances of single-origin Indian coffees.

Whether you are stepping into a third-wave cafe for the first time or want to expand your daily coffee routine, navigating a contemporary specialty menu can feel daunting. Here is our curated guide to the five best specialty coffee drinks to try in Pune, including how they taste, when to drink them, and what makes them special.

1. The Espresso Tonic: Pune's Ultimate Afternoon Reviver

When afternoon temperatures in Pune climb during April and May, hot espresso drinks lose their appeal. Enter the Espresso Tonic. While the combination of tonic water and freshly pulled espresso might sound unusual to beginners, it has quickly become one of the most requested drinks in specialty coffee shops across Maharashtra.

The secret lies in contrast. The bitter quinine and effervescence of chilled botanical tonic water cut right through the rich, oily density of a double espresso shot. When paired with a light-to-medium roast arabica—such as a washed lot from Chikkamagaluru—the drink yields sparkling notes of grapefruit, sweet lime, and brown sugar.

Best For: Warm afternoons (between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM) when you need refreshing clarity without the heaviness of milk. Check our Espresso Bar Menu to see our house recipe.

2. The Cortado (Gibraltar): Precision Milk-to-Coffee Ratio

If you love milk coffee but find regular lattes and cappuccinos overly diluted with warm dairy, the Cortado is the golden ratio. Originating in Spain and popularized by specialty roasters across California, a Cortado consists of equal parts double espresso and warm, textured micro-foam (typically 1:1, served in a 4-ounce glass).

Unlike a flat white or latte where milk dominates the flavor profile, a Cortado allows the nuanced chocolate, hazelnut, and fruit undertones of the espresso to shine brightly. Because it is served warm rather than piping hot, you can drink it immediately without scalding your palate.

"A well-extracted Cortado is the true litmus test of any specialty cafe. With only two ounces of milk, there is nowhere for flawed espresso to hide."

3. Single-Origin V60 Pour-Over: The Slow Bar Purist

For those who want to experience coffee as a complex agricultural fruit rather than a dark roasty beverage, nothing surpasses a manual pour-over. Using a cone-shaped Hario V60 dripper with paper filtration, hot mineral-balanced water is poured over freshly ground beans in calculated spirals.

In Pune's cooler monsoon and winter mornings, ordering a pour-over prepared with beans from high-altitude Indian estates is an experience in mindfulness. You will detect delicate aromatic layers resembling jasmine, stone fruit, and bergamot. If you are curious about bean genetics and roast profiles, read our companion guide on How to Choose Coffee Beans.

4. Kyoto-Style Cold Drip: Patience in a Glass

Standard iced coffees are often made by dumping hot espresso over melting ice cubes, which shocks the coffee and dilutes flavor. In contrast, Kyoto-style cold drip brews over 12 to 18 hours using icy water dripping at a rate of one drop per second through a column of ground coffee.

Because cold water does not extract chlorogenic acids and harsh tannins the way near-boiling water does, cold drip coffee has virtually zero bitterness and a syrupy, liqueur-like mouthfeel. To explore the science behind this process, read our deep-dive comparison: Coffee vs Cold Brew: Extraction & Chemistry.

5. The Specialty Flat White: Silky Micro-Foam Perfection

The flat white was perfected in Australasia and has become a global standard in third-wave cafes. Prepared with a double ristretto shot and velvety steamed milk with virtually no dry froth, it delivers intense coffee flavor with the smoothest texture imaginable.

Pairing a flat white with a warm, flaky French croissant or fresh sourdough toast in the morning creates one of the simplest culinary pleasures. Explore our complete morning kitchen in our All-Day Breakfast Menu.
