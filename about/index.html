Independent Roastery & Cafe

Our Story: Championing Single-Origin Indian Coffee in Pune

Founded on a simple premise: Indian shade-grown arabica coffee rivals the finest lots in the world when respected at farm, roast, and cup level.

How Pune Brew House Came to Life

Pune has always been a city of thinkers, writers, engineers, and creatives. For decades, its cafe culture centered around heritage Irani cafes and sweet milky filter coffees. While we deeply cherish those traditions, we felt Pune deserved a sanctuary dedicated to the nuances of contemporary third-wave specialty coffee.

We established Pune Brew House to strip away pretension. You don't need a certificate in sensory evaluation to enjoy a cup here. Whether you walk in for a rich morning flat white or want to talk to our baristas about grind distribution on a light roast pour-over, you will be met with warmth and curiosity.

Our space in Koregaon Park was built with deliberate acoustics, plenty of natural light, and ample space between tables so remote workers, book lovers, and conversationalists can coexist harmoniously.

Direct Trade Partnerships

Respecting the Western Ghats Terroir

India produces some of the most ecologically diverse coffee in the world, grown under multi-tier canopies of wild fig, silver oak, and jackfruit trees.

Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka

Elevation: 1,200m – 1,450m | Varietal: SLN 79.5 & Chandragiri

Known as the birthplace of coffee in India, these high-elevation lots produce cups with velvety body, gentle orange blossom aromatics, and notes of milk chocolate.

Coorg (Kodagu), Karnataka

Elevation: 1,100m – 1,350m | Varietal: S.795 Washed

Grown alongside black pepper and cardamom plantations, this lot brings subtle spice undertones, caramelized apple sweetness, and balanced malic acidity.

Araku Valley, Andhra Pradesh

Elevation: 1,100m | Process: Yeast Macerated Natural

An experimental lot processed by tribal farmer cooperatives, yielding punchy berry notes, dried dates, and a winey, full-bodied finish.

Behind the Bar

Precision Brewing Without the Pretense

Every morning before we open our doors at 7:30 AM, our baristas dial in the grinders. Weather, humidity, and degassing continuously shift how coffee extracts. We measure total dissolved solids (TDS) and adjust grind microns daily to ensure your espresso never tastes sour or bitter.

We also run an eco-conscious cafe. We encourage patrons to bring reusable tumblers for takeaway orders and repurpose 100% of our spent coffee grounds with local community urban gardeners across Pune.
