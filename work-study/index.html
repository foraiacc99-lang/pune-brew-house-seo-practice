Productive Workspace

Work & Study Friendly Specialty Cafe in Pune

Whether you are writing your thesis, building a startup, or tackling freelance projects, we have created an environment where productivity and craft coffee meet.

A Cafe Designed for Focus, Not Hustle Culture

Many cafes in Pune either blast loud commercial EDM or treat laptop users as an inconvenience. At Pune Brew House, we intentionally planned our space to embrace students, freelancers, and remote teams.

We invested in commercial acoustic dampening, custom-built solid teakwood communal tables with recessed wire management, and large casement windows that bring in natural daylight without screen glare.

Our Work & Study Etiquette

To keep our doors open and ensure fellow patrons can also enjoy a seat, we ask everyone to honor a few simple community courtesies:

  • Fair Table Sharing: During lunch hours (12:30 PM – 2:30 PM), please refrain from spreading books across multi-seat tables.
  • Audio Etiquette: Always wear headphones for audio/video playback. If you have an extended voice call, please take it on our outdoor garden patio.
  • Mindful Consumption: We do not charge hourly desk fees. We politely request ordering a beverage, snack, or meal for every 2 to 3 hours you spend working with us.

Frequently Asked Questions: Working at Pune Brew House

Is the Wi-Fi at Pune Brew House suitable for video calls?

Yes. We maintain a dual-band enterprise fiber connection delivering average download speeds of 150 Mbps and low latency, perfectly suited for Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and large code pushes.

Are there charging points for laptops and phones?

Every communal table and window counter seat has built-in universal power sockets and USB ports. We also keep spare iPhone and USB-C laptop chargers behind the counter if you forget yours.

What are the quietest hours for deep study and focused work?

The quietest hours are weekday mornings between 7:30 AM and 11:30 AM, and weekday afternoons between 2:30 PM and 5:00 PM. Weekends are generally more social and conversational.

Do you offer discounts for college students?

Yes! Students from colleges across Pune (such as Symbiosis, Fergusson, COEP, BMCC, etc.) receive a 10% discount on all coffee beverages upon presenting a valid student ID card.
