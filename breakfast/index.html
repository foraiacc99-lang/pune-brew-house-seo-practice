Slow Fermentation

The 36-Hour Sourdough Foundation

Every piece of toast we serve begins with heritage grains, water, salt, and a living wild yeast culture nurtured for years. Fermented for 36 hours before baking, the sourdough has a crunchy caramelized crust, an open chewy crumb, and a gentle tang that perfectly cuts through rich butter or ripe avocado.

Because the gluten is broken down during the long fermentation process, it is lighter on digestion and creates a balanced foundation for high-energy mornings.
